Most of what I build sits between a business and the services it depends on. A customer orders on WhatsApp, a driver gets the job on their phone, the payment comes in through mobile money or the bank, and an SMS confirms it. I enjoy making all of those pieces work together.

I'm comfortable with the parts that aren't code too: sitting with the business to understand what they need, explaining technical choices to people who aren't technical, and keeping a system healthy after launch.

Before moving fully into software I spent five years at MITz Group managing projects and teams, and starting STEM programmes so students could learn by building things.
