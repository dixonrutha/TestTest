About

Today I'm Head of Systems at ChemCheemi Africa, where I look after our systems, our IoT platform TapCloud and our AI work. I also build software for Ecowater International, including the cloud system we launched in Uganda and Malawi.

Most of what I build sits between a business and the services it depends on. A customer orders water on WhatsApp, a driver gets the delivery on their phone, the payment comes in through mobile money or the bank, and an SMS confirms it. I like owning that whole chain, from the first conversation about what the business needs to keeping the system running after launch.

Before this I spent five years at MITz Group in Mwanza, managing projects and teams and starting STEM programmes for students. Since 2019 I've also built websites and custom systems for clients on the side.

What I connect

The pieces I've connected across ChemCheemi, Ecowater and Gasify. Data from stores, meters and chat comes into one cloud system, and payments and messages go out through local banks, mobile money and SMS.
